# Key Dental Health Indicators for Public Education (2026)

**A sourced compilation with derived comparisons, prepared for public dental-health education.**

Compiled by Masood Ahmad, Editor, Dentist in Pico Rivera (dentalpicorivera.com), November 2026.
Companion page: https://dentalpicorivera.com/dental-health-statistics/

## What this dataset is

`dental-health-indicators-2026.csv` brings together the key dental-health indicators most often needed by educators, journalists and community health workers, each quoted from its primary publisher (WHO, CDC) with the source title, URL and access date recorded for every row. `derived-comparisons-2026.csv` contains five short comparisons computed directly from those compiled figures, each labelled with the rows it derives from.

This is a **curated compilation and secondary analysis of published figures** — it is not new clinical research, and no figure in it originates with the compiler. Its value is provenance and usability: every number can be traced to the named primary source in one step.

## Methodology

1. **Source selection.** Only primary publishers of population health statistics were used: the World Health Organization (global burden) and the U.S. Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (United States). No secondary blogs, commercial roundups or unsourced statistics pages were used.
2. **Inclusion criteria.** An indicator was included if it (a) describes prevalence, burden, prevention effect or economic cost of dental disease; (b) is stated as a headline figure by the source itself; and (c) can be quoted exactly without methodological caveats that a short note cannot carry.
3. **Extraction.** Values were extracted on 4 November 2026 from the source pages listed in `source_url`. Where a source states a figure in words ("1 in 5"), the dataset records both the wording and the numeric equivalent in the `unit`/`notes` fields.
4. **Derived comparisons.** The five comparisons in the second file are arithmetic or juxtaposition only (ratios, pairings, effect sizes placed side by side). No modelling, estimation or extrapolation was performed, and no derived figure is presented as a source figure.
5. **Verification.** Each extracted figure was cross-checked against the corresponding figure as presented on the companion statistics page, which was itself compiled from the same primary pages on the same date.

## Limitations

- Figures carry the reference periods of their sources (e.g. GBD 2021 for the global ranking); they are the latest headline figures published by each source at the access date, not measurements taken in 2026.
- Percentages from different surveys (notably the CDC child indicators) describe overlapping but non-identical populations; the derived comparisons flag this where relevant.
- If a source updates or withdraws a figure, the affected row should be corrected or removed at the next review.
